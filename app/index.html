Questions

I paid but the subject site still looks locked.

Reload the subject site once. If it is still locked, sign in on this page and press "Check my payment" below the subject. If money was taken, access is added automatically, and you can write to notesbugsreportandsupport@gmail.com with your payment ID.

Which account should I use?

Your VIT student Google account, on both this page and the subject site. Access is personal and tied to that email.

Is this run by VIT?

No. The notes are made by a student to help with studying and are not official VIT material.
