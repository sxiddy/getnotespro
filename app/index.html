Help

Questions

Do I have to pay to read the notes?

No. The notes for every module are free to read on the subject sites. Pro only adds the study tools on top.

I paid but the subject site still looks locked.

Reload the subject site once. If it is still locked, sign in on this page and press "Check my payment" below. If money was taken, access is added automatically, and you can write to notesbugsreportandsupport@gmail.com with your payment ID.

Which account should I use?

Your VIT student Google account, on both this page and the subject site. Access is personal and tied to that email.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Everything works in a phone browser, and your progress, bookmarks and notes sync between your phone and laptop when you sign in with the same account.

Is paying here safe?

Payment is handled by Razorpay over UPI. This site never sees your bank or UPI details, only a confirmation that the payment went through.

Is this run by VIT?

No. The notes are made by a student to help with studying and are not official VIT material.

Can I get a refund?

See the refund and cancellation policy.
