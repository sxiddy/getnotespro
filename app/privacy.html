Privacy

Notes Pro is a small store run by a student. This page explains what it stores about you and why. Last updated 6 October 2026.

What is stored

  • Your VIT student email, from "Sign in with Google". It is used to let you in and to link your payments to your account. Your name and profile photo from Google are not stored.
  • When you first signed in, when you last signed in, and how many times, shown to the owner.
  • Your payment record for each subject: the Razorpay payment ID, amount and date, linked to your email. UPI details are handled entirely by Razorpay; this website never sees your bank or UPI details.
  • Which subjects you bought, which the subject websites check (by your email) to unlock Pro for you.

What is not done

  • Your data is not sold or shared with anyone, and not used for ads.
  • No tracking or advertising cookies are used. The only cookies keep you signed in and remember which subject you came from for a few minutes.

Where it is kept

The website runs on Vercel, with a small Upstash database for the records above. Payments go through Razorpay. Google handles sign-in.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To have your email and payment record deleted, write to notesbugsreportandsupport@gmail.com from your student account. Deleting your record also removes your Pro access.
